# FinTechathon — Past Proposals and Pitch Deck References

Research date: 2026-10-19. These references were researched for AegisPath's entry in the **2026 International Track, Topic C: Cross-border & Student Finance Assistant**.

## Competition context

The theme is **AI as a Financial Participant**: demonstrate an agent completing financial tasks, not merely answering questions. The supplied International Track rules weight task completion at **40%**, security and compliance at **30%**, and innovation and interaction at **30%**. Presentations must be in English and no longer than **10 minutes**.

**2026 is the first International Track**, as confirmed by the [IEEE CIS announcement](https://r10.ieee.org/shenzhen-cis/blog/2026/06/17/2026-shenzhen-international-fintech-competition-international-track/). Earlier materials below are from other FinTechathon tracks, not previous International Track entries.

English project names below are descriptive translations, not necessarily official branding. Most source documents are in Chinese; renaming the files does not translate their contents.

## Applying these references to AegisPath

Lead with a concrete student journey rather than a broad platform label:

> A student needs to pay tuition across currencies. AegisPath interprets the request, plans the operation, obtains the required approval, executes in the sandbox, and provides an auditable result. A malicious invoice or altered instruction must not redirect the payment.

This is a recommended narrative, not a claim that all behavior has been verified. Demonstrate the actual implementation and distinguish sandbox behavior from real payment integration.

Use the references to improve:

1. **Problem and value:** one specific student financial problem and its consequences.
2. **Task completion:** observable end-to-end workflow, including tools and execution evidence.
3. **Security:** trust boundaries, authorization, sensitive-action confirmation, and attack handling.
4. **Interaction:** clarification, failures, and recovery—not just a happy-path chat.
5. **Evaluation:** measured results with methodology and limitations, not borrowed metrics.

## Corrections and submission cautions

- **Do not identify `effective-p/FinAgent` as the 2025 winning submission.** [That repository](https://github.com/effective-p/FinAgent) describes a trading-agent implementation associated with a 2024 research paper. Its identity with the 2025 “one piece” competition entry was not established. The [HKUST(GZ) report](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/zh/?p=13630%2F) confirms the competition project's third prize and personal-financial-management scope, but does not establish that repository link.
- The local competition summary labels source code, security assessment, and execution evidence optional. The supplied [International Track rules](../competition/referensi_lomba/international-topics-rules-en.docx) list them without that qualification. Only the standalone demo-video item is explicitly marked optional; execution evidence separately mentions logs and video. Do not rely on the summary's optional labels.
- These are references, not reusable submission text. Respect intellectual property, attribute borrowed ideas, and check licenses before reusing code or assets.
